Everyone gets a pane on the left with clickable options like:
	1. Employees View (Default landing page post-login):
        - clickable cards for all employees with
            - Employee Profile Picture
            - Employee Name
            - Employee status dot (green for present today in office, airplane icon for on leave, yellow dot for absent without applied time off)
            - On-click
                - Employee info page in view-only mode
        - Profile picture for logged in user that shows my profile and log out buttons
        - red status dot for their own check in that shows a systray when clicked with check in/check out buttons that change the red status dot to green and back.

	2a. Attendance View (For Admin/HR/Payroll Officer):
        - table with attendance of all employees present on a choosable particular date and day with Employee Names, check in check out times, working hours and extra hours. default date is today
        - search bar
        Parts that wont be visible in this view but are important for database design
            - Attendance data serves as the basis for payslip generation.
            - Use the attendance records to determine the number of payable days for each employee
            - Unpaid leave or missing attendance days should automatically reduce the number of payable days during payslip computation

    2b. Attendance View (For Employees):
        -  Choosing Month shows week-wise sections with their own attendance tables that show date of the week, check in check out times, work hours and extra hours for that day. Three fields at the top show count of days present for that month, count of leaves, total number of working days

	3a. Time Off View (For Admin & HR Officer):
        - Show searchbar
        - button to create new time off with modal that shows details like dropdown of employee names to choose from, time off type (paid or sick or unpaid leave), validity period (date range (no limit if end date is not given)), allocation (number of days(what to do if end date not given?)), attachment upload (for sick leave certificate)
        - Table of requested time offs with Name of the Employee that requested it, start date, end date, time off type, status of time off (approved/rejected/pending approval) and reject and approve buttons
        - Remaining Paid time off and sick time off days in the month e.g. 24 days available paid time off and 7 days available sick time off
	3b. Time Off View (For Employee):
        - button to request new time off with modal that shows details like employee name, time off type (paid or sick or unpaid leave), validity period (date range), allocation (number of days), attachment upload (for sick leave certificate)
        - Table of their own requested time offs with their name, start date, end date, time off type, status of of time off (approved/rejected/pending approval) and reject and approve buttons
        - Remaining Paid time off and sick time off days in the month
	
    4. Payroll View (only for Admin and Payroll Officer):
        - Dashboard Tab:    
            - graph of employer cost annually and monthly (toggle)
            - graph of employee count annually and monthly (toggle)
            - warnings mentioned above (employee count without bank a/c, without manager)
            - month-wise payrun option with payslip count
        - Payrun Tab (generate payslips for all employees at once):
            - Payrun button that generates all payslips automatically
            - Payrun overview section shows Payrun of a month with employer cost, gross wage and net wage for that month
            - Pay table that shows
                - Pay period
                - Employee Name
                - Employer Cost (employee's monthly wage)
                - Basic Wage (employee's basic salary)
                - Gross Wage (total of basic salary + allowances)
                - Net wage (gross wage minus deductions)
                - Status ("Done" if a payrun or payslip has been validated)
            - When opening a generated payslip in the pay table:
                - Employee Name 
                - Payrun month
                - Salary structure (usually "regular pay")
                - Period (1st and last date of month)
                - Buttons
                    - New Payslip
                    - Compute
                    - Validate
                    - Cancel
                    - Print PDF
                        - company logo
                        - Month of salary slip
                        - Section with employee name, code, dept, location, date of joining, PAN, UAN, Bank A/C No, Pay Period, Pay Date
                        - Section with worked days type, number of days and totals
                        - Section with earnings amounts, and deductions amounts
                        - Total Net Payable (Gross Earning - Total Deductions) Amount in words and numbers
                - Tabs
                    - Worked Days Tab (table view)
                        - Type (attendance, paid time off, etc.)
                        - Days (number of attendance days, paid time off, etc.)
                        - Amount earned for those days
                            - Salary calculated based on the employee's payable days (monthly attendance + paid leaves) - unpaid leaves
                        - Totals row with gross amount and number of days
                    - Salary Computation Tab (Table View)
                        - Rule Name (Basic Salary, allowances, Bonuses, gross, deductions (PF, Tax))
                        - Rate %
                        - Amount
                        - Net amount row

	5. Reports (For Admin/Payroll Officers only)
    - Choose employee name and year
    - Print PDF button for salary statement of that employee and that year
    - PDF shows:
        - "Salary Statement Report" with Section with Company Name, Employee Name, Designation, Date of Joining, Salary Effective From
        - Table with columns:
            - Salary Components (Earnings and Deductions)
            - Monthly Amount
            - Yearly Amount
            - Row of Net salary amounts

	6. Settings (admin only):
	- Table where admin can
		- view users' names, login IDs
		- edit emails and roles of users (User Type field)
